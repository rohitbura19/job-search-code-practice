# Warm-up (10 min, blank page)

1. `thirdMax` with `Integer.MIN_VALUE` in the input: why is `int` as the "empty" marker a bug, and what sentinel is safe?
2. Floyd's cycle detection: prove that restarting one pointer at the head and stepping both by one meets at the cycle entrance. Why does `findDuplicate` on `[1..n]` + one repeat have a cycle at all?
3. House Robber III: write the two values returned per node. Why does a plain `rob(node)` recursion without them go exponential?
4. Skyline: why must start events sort before end events at the same x, and taller starts first? What breaks otherwise (name one input)?
5. `@Inheritance(SINGLE_TABLE)`: why must subclass columns be nullable, and what does `type(n) = EmailNotification` compile to?
