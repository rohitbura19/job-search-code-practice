# F2. HLD: wallet-to-wallet transfer with idempotency and a ledger

Feature of a payments super-app: a user moves money from their wallet to another user's wallet instantly. Requirements: exactly-once effect even when the client retries or the network drops after commit; no negative balances; auditable history; balance and statement reads are fast; 3,000 transfers/s peak; multi-currency is out of scope.

Cover in 25 minutes: API (`POST /transfers` with `Idempotency-Key`, status codes, async vs sync), data model (accounts, double-entry ledger entries, transfers, idempotency table; why balance is derived or a checked cache), the transaction (single database: row lock order to avoid deadlock `ORDER BY account_id`, optimistic version vs `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`; sharded database: why a 2PC is avoided and the saga / outbox with a pending state), hot accounts (a merchant receiving 1,000 credits/s), the idempotency race (two identical requests at once: unique constraint, stored response), reconciliation and invariants (sum of ledger = 0), events to downstream (outbox + Kafka, ordering by account), read model for statements (CQRS, keyset pagination), failure drills (crash between debit and credit, duplicate Kafka delivery), fraud limits, trade-offs. Draw it in `my-designs/F2-wallet-transfer.md`.
