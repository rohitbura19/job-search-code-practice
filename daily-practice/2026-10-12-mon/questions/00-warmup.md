## 0) Warm-up: spaced repetition (5 min)

From a blank page, no peeking, a few minutes each:

1. Reverse a singly linked list iteratively (save `next`, flip, advance both). Say out loud where the cycle bug hides in the recursive version.
2. The lower-bound binary search template: `lo = 0, hi = n` (exclusive), `mid = lo + (hi - lo) / 2`, `if (a[mid] < target) lo = mid + 1 else hi = mid`. What does it return when the target is larger than everything?
3. One sentence each, out loud: why a max-heap for the lower half and a min-heap for the upper half gives the median; why union-find needs path compression AND union by size.

Spaced repetition from the sheet: re-attempt anything from Sun 11 Oct marked "needed hints" today, and mark anything from today that needed hints for Wed 14 Oct and Mon 19 Oct.
