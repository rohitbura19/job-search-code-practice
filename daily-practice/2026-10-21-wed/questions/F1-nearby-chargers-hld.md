## F) HLD — two small features of a big app (30 min, about 15 each)

Use this frame: requirements (functional, non-functional, scale numbers) -> API -> data model -> high-level diagram -> deep dive on the one hard part -> failure modes -> metrics. (IDFC R2 is 60 minutes of DB + HLD + LLD, and the Light & Wonder interview will have a design discussion: practise saying the frame in the first two minutes.)

### F1. "Find a free fast charger near me" — nearby public chargers with live availability
The app shows a map and a list of public chargers around the driver (or around the map area), filtered by connector type, minimum power (for example 50 kW or more), "only free now", price and operator. The pins must show the live state (free / in use / faulted / unknown) and update while the map is open. Rules: the first result within 300 ms; status no older than about 30 s; a charger whose state we cannot vouch for must not be shown as free. Scale: 150k public connectors at about 60k stations, 1M app users a day, searches (including every map pan and zoom) peak at about 5k/s, status changes about 250/s on average and 1k/s at the evening peak, a few "hot" cities where most searches happen.

Think about: separating STATIC data (location, connectors, power, amenities; changes rarely) from DYNAMIC data (status; changes constantly) because they have opposite caching needs; the numbers (60k stations at about 100 bytes is 6 MB, the live status of 150k connectors is a few MB: does the whole thing fit in the memory of every API pod?); a spatial index (geohash / S2 / H3 cells, PostGIS, or a grid in memory) and what "near" costs; k-nearest versus "inside this map box"; tiles and clustering at low zoom (counts per cell) versus pins at high zoom; derived station status (`free_count`, `best_free_kw`) computed once per change, not per query; where the status comes from (OCPP StatusNotification, heartbeats, third-party roaming feeds with lag), debouncing flaps, and a TTL after which a silent charger becomes UNKNOWN rather than FREE; push to the open map (WebSocket / SSE by cell) versus polling; CDN caching of tiles with a short TTL; hot cities; the honest "updated 40 s ago"; reserving a free charger (the 20 Oct reservation design) as the answer to "it was free when I left"; metrics.
