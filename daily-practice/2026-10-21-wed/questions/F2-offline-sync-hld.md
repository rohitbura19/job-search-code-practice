### F2. Offline-first commissioning checklist for installers
An installer commissions a home or site charger on the spot: about 40 checklist items (tick boxes, readings such as voltage and earth resistance, notes, scanned serial numbers), 10 to 20 photos, a customer signature, then "submit". The site is often a basement garage with no signal; the installer may work for hours offline, sometimes with a helper on a second phone, and a supervisor may correct fields from the web console later. Rules: nothing the installer typed is ever lost; the same data is never applied twice; photos upload in the background and survive a dead battery; after "submit" the record is locked and audited; the app must keep working with an old version for a while. Scale: 40k installers, about 5,000 commissionings a day (150k a month), about 10 photos of 3 MB each per job (about 150 GB a day), the typical job syncs several times.

Think about: a local database on the phone and an operation LOG (outbox) instead of "save the form"; operations with a client-generated id (idempotent replay), a device id and a per-device sequence; the batch sync API and a pull of changes since a server revision; conflict rules per field type (last writer wins for a scalar, add-only sets for photos and notes, server-authoritative state for submit/lock) and which clock decides (never the phone's wall clock alone); photos on a separate resumable path (presigned multipart upload, content hash, metadata first and bytes later); expired tokens while offline; schema versions; what happens on logout, reinstall or a lost phone with unsynced work; battery and data cost; privacy of customer photos and signatures; metrics.
