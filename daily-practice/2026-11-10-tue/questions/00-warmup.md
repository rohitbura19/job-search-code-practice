# Warm-up (10 min, blank page)

1. Two pointers on a sorted array with negatives: why does filling the result from the back with the larger absolute value give sorted squares in O(n)?
2. Bipartite check: what exactly does BFS 2-colouring detect, and why is it equivalent to "no odd cycle"? Why must you loop over all start nodes?
3. Stock with cooldown: name the three states and write the three transitions. Which one lags a day behind?
4. Largest rectangle in a histogram: when a bar is popped from the monotonic stack, what are its left and right boundaries, and why does each bar produce its best rectangle exactly once?
5. `@ManyToOne(fetch = LAZY)`: what does `findById(...).getAccount().getEmail()` need outside a transaction, and what does `@EntityGraph` change in the generated SQL?
