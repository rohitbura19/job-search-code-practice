### A2. Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (Greedy, one pass)
`prices[i]` is the price on day i. Pick one day to buy and a later day to sell. Return the maximum profit, or 0. `[7,1,5,3,6,4] -> 5`; `[7,6,4,3,1] -> 0`.

Interviewer follow-up: unlimited transactions? At most k transactions? With a cooldown day? The prices arrive as a stream and you cannot store them — what do you keep?
