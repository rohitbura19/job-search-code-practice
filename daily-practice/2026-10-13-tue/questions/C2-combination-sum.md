### C2. Combination Sum (Backtracking)
`candidates` (distinct positive ints, each reusable any number of times) and `target`. Return all unique combinations summing to `target`. `[2,3,6,7], 7 -> [[2,2,3],[7]]`; `[2,3,5], 8 -> [[2,2,2,2],[2,3,3],[3,5]]`.

Interviewer follow-up: Combination Sum II where candidates have duplicates and each is used once — how do you avoid duplicate answers? Only the COUNT is needed and target is 10^4 — what do you switch to? What is the worst-case complexity?
