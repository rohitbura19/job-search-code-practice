# Warm-up (10 min, blank page, no IDE)

Spaced repetition from the last two weeks. State the invariant or recurrence in one sentence each; mark "needed hints" in the score card.

1. Binary search on a rotated array (10-24): what does the sorted half tell you at each step?
2. Sliding window with a deque (10-11 / 10-22): what does the deque hold, and when is the front evicted?
3. Union-find (10-25): path compression + union by size; what is the amortised cost?
4. Write `Node pop()` for a min-heap by hand: sift-down, the two child comparisons.
5. `HashMap.get` on a key whose `hashCode` changed after insertion: where does it look (you will meet this in H1)?
