# Warm-up (10 min, blank page, no IDE)

1. Boyer-Moore majority vote: state the invariant in one sentence. Why does it need the "a majority exists" guarantee, and what would a second pass add?
2. Kahn's algorithm: how do you detect a cycle with no extra DFS, and why does a min-heap instead of a queue give the lexicographically smallest order?
3. LIS with tails[]: what does tails[k] mean, why is it always sorted, and what changes if the subsequence may be non-decreasing?
4. A delayed-job scheduler stores jobs in a min-heap by due time. What does a worker do when the heap top is 40 s away, and how does a new earlier job wake it up?
