## E) Transactional Outbox + Kafka retry/DLT + JWT security + metrics (25 min)

**Task.** Complete the TODOs without an IDE. A site alerting service. `POST /api/v1/alerts` (requires a JWT with scope `alerts:write`) saves an `Alert` and an `OutboxEvent` in ONE database transaction. A relay job publishes unsent outbox rows to Kafka topic `alerts` keyed by `siteId`, safely across several pods. A consumer processes alerts with retries on a retry topic and a dead-letter topic. Spring Boot 3, Java 17+, Postgres.
